Same faceted low-poly language as the winning tree canopy (sketch 001): angular polygon panels, brass/copper gradients, small gold gear rivets as a recurring accent. Machines read as "mechanical siblings" of the trees.

B: Grounded Steampunk Machinery — boilers, rivets, pistons

A more literal industrial-steampunk read: riveted cylindrical boiler tanks, piston rods, smokestacks with puffing smoke. Heavier, more "real machine" than variant A's stylized facets.

Rounded, simplified shapes with big friendly wheels and soft warm color blocks (still brass/copper-adjacent, not a full palette swap) — leans toward the "Beavers City" friendliness direction raised earlier in this project's design discussion, applied to machines and workers rather than the already-locked tree style.
