<objective>
Make the season a real factor in sale price, on all three copies of the formula that exist today: the Motoko pure functions, the TypeScript mirror, and the modal the player reads before confirming a sale.

Purpose: `seasonMultiplier` has shipped as a literal `1.0` with a `// TODO: Vary by season` comment. The game's own AI demand curve already says Summer is peak and Winter is trough, so the market the AI competes in and the market the player sells into have never been the same market.

Output: one Motoko season function, one TS mirror pinned by the parity oracle, both price functions parameterised, the Candid `MarketPrice.seasonMultiplier` field telling the truth, and a sale preview that quotes the number the backend will actually pay.
</objective>
