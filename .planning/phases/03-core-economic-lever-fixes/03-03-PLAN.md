<objective>
Make the auction runner-up trickle real on the AI side: compute it where the reserved constants already sit, carry it on `AuctionResult`, and credit an AI second-place bidder in the global resolution loop.

Purpose: `auction_logic.mo` has carried `runnerUpId` and a 20/100 trickle constant pair since it was written, both explicitly marked "reserved for future full trickle impl". The payout was never computed, so second place has always been worth exactly nothing. This plan proves the whole computation end-to-end on the one path that needs no new persisted state, retiring the formula risk before plan 03-04 spends a decision gate on the player path.

Output: a computed, returned and credited trickle payout; a transcription test that pins the formula and its bidder-count boundaries.
</objective>
