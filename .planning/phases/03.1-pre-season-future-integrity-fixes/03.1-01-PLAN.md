<objective>
Fix ECON-08: `resolveSeasonAuctions` checks every one of a caller's committed Pre-Season
Future contracts against the same pre-loop `totalDelivered` snapshot instead of the pool
that is actually left after earlier contracts in the same resolution pass already consumed
some of it. A player holding more than one PSF in a season (nothing limits how many, and
`advancePhase` mints one Export + one Bio + one Industrial PSF every season) evades a large
fraction of the intended shortfall/buyback/default exposure.

Purpose: the auction system's economics are only as real as its shortfall accounting. Today
a player who commits to Export (10,000 kg) and Industrial (20,000 kg) and delivers 12,000 kg
is penalised for 8,000 kg of shortfall instead of 18,000 kg — a ~10,000 kg free pass bought
by doing nothing more exotic than signing two contracts.

Output: a running accumulator threaded through the PSF resolution branch of
`backend/main.mo`, proven on a live replica with before/after shortfall numbers, plus a new
transcription-oracle + source-drift-guard test file that pins the arithmetic and its
boundaries.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| player principal -> `resolveSeasonAuctions` (update call) | A caller controls when resolution runs, how many PSFs they hold, and (via gameplay) how much stock is in inventory at that moment. The arithmetic inside the call is the only thing enforcing the contract's economics. |
| resolution arithmetic -> stable state (`stableAuctionContracts`, `playerFarms`) | Values computed in the loop are persisted; a wrong number becomes durable player-visible cash/debt/reputation. |
