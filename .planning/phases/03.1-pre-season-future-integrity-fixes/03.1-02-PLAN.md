<objective>
Fix ECON-09: `commitPreSeasonFuture(contractId, volumeKg)` uses the caller-supplied `volumeKg`
only to compute the 10% commitment fee. It is never compared against, nor written into,
`contract.requiredVolumeKg` — the fixed delivery obligation the player is actually held to at
resolution time. A direct canister call with `volumeKg = 1` pays a fee that truncates to 0 PLN
for every realistic locked price (5-17 PLN/kg) and still locks in the discounted price and the
full obligation. The frontend happens to pass the right value today; the frontend is not a
trust boundary.

Purpose: close a free-lock-in exploit reachable by anyone who can call the canister directly,
without changing a Candid-visible signature (which would be an Ask-First contract-surface
change under CLAUDE.md).

Output: an additive equality guard in `backend/main.mo`, the fee sourced from
`contract.requiredVolumeKg`, proof on a live replica that all four volume cases behave
correctly, and proof that `frontend/src/declarations/` did not move a byte.
</objective>
