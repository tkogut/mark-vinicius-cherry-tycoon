**Threaded a running `remainingForPSF` accumulator through `resolveSeasonAuctions`' Pre-Season Future branch, closing a ~10,000 kg penalty-evasion hole for players holding multiple PSFs in one season, verified before/after on a live dfx replica.**

## Accomplishments

- Fixed CR-01 (ECON-08): `resolveSeasonAuctions` now checks each of a caller's committed PSF contracts against the pool actually left after earlier contracts in the same resolution pass, not a stale pre-loop snapshot. Verified live: the CR-01 worked example (Export 10,000 kg + Industrial 20,000 kg required, 12,000 kg delivered) moved from a combined shortfall of 8,000 kg to the correct 18,000 kg.
- Added a hand-maintained transcription oracle + source drift guard (`preSeasonFutureIntegrity.test.ts`, 14 tests) that pins the accumulator's arithmetic, its boundaries (zero-PSF, zero-delivered, exact-match, over-commitment, cross-player isolation, order-dependence), and its literal presence in `backend/main.mo`.
- Proved both the oracle and the drift guard actually catch the bug by reverting each in turn and observing the expected, isolated failure — then restoring both before committing.
