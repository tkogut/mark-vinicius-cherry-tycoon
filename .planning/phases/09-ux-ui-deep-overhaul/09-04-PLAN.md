<objective>
Establish UX-03's two-pattern stat grammar (D-04) as real, reusable primitives, and migrate the bottom HUD — which `09-RESEARCH.md` confirms is the audit's literal "Diagnostics panel" and the app's de facto bottom dock — into D-00's consolidated **Action Dock**, taking D-11's two dead controls with it.

Purpose: the audit's complaint is that stat displays mix a circular gauge, vertical tube gauges and plain text with no shared grammar. The fix is not restyling; it is deciding which shape means what (bounded -> Bar Meter, open-ended -> Metric Badge) and then having exactly two shapes.

Output: two new UI primitives, a `brass` Badge variant, a rewritten `ActionDock.tsx` (renamed from `MainDashboard.tsx`), real navigation behind the auction CTA, and an app-wide ratchet that keeps circular gauges out of passive stat displays permanently.
</objective>
